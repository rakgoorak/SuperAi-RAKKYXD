# Privacy Policy — SuperAi&RAKKYXD

Effective date: November 12, 2026

This policy applies to the Microsoft Store version of SuperAi&RAKKYXD, starting
with version 1.0.33.0, published by SuperAi&RAKKYXD.

## Information accessed and stored

The app accesses project folders you select, source code, file paths, prompts,
AI responses, terminal commands and their output, and files you attach. These
may contain personal or confidential information. The app uses this information
to provide coding assistance, project editing, terminal sessions, and conversation
history.

App-managed conversation history, project references, settings, and diagnostic
logs are stored on your computer, normally under `%USERPROFILE%\.opencode-multi`,
and in the app's local browser storage. Attachments are saved under
`.superai/attachments` in the relevant project. Your project source files remain
in the folders you select. Developer CLI tools may keep additional data and
credentials in their own local storage.

The built-in browser may store cookies, website storage, and session information
locally. Websites you visit process information according to their own policies.

## AI services and other third parties

When you use a connected AI provider or CLI tool, your requests and relevant
project context, which can include source code, attachments, file paths, and
command output, are sent to that provider. The information included depends on
your request, configuration, and the files or tools accessed during the task.
Connected MCP servers and other tools may also receive information needed for
their functions.

You choose the provider and use your own provider account or configuration.
Provider authentication and credentials are handled by the relevant developer
tools. Provider policies determine their retention, processing locations,
training practices, and account controls. Using a local Ollama server keeps that
model's inference on the machine hosting the server; a remote server receives
the requests you send to it.

The app's publisher does not operate an intermediary server for your AI
conversations. This Store build does not include publisher-operated usage
analytics or advertising trackers. Third-party CLI tools and services may have
their own telemetry settings.

Installing tools or skills, searching skill catalogs, and downloading speech
models can contact services such as GitHub, npm, Node.js distribution hosts,
skills.sh, and Hugging Face. These services receive network information such as
your IP address and the requested resource; searches may include your search
terms. Microsoft handles Store installation and updates under Microsoft's own
privacy policy.

## Microphone

Voice dictation accesses your microphone when you activate recording and grant
permission. Speech recognition runs locally using downloaded models; the
dictation feature does not upload raw microphone audio for transcription.
If you submit the resulting text to an AI provider, that text is shared with
the selected provider as part of your request.

## Retention, security, and your choices

Locally saved data remains until you delete it or the app or relevant tool
removes it. Closing a conversation may remove its app-managed saved session;
this does not delete records retained by a provider or CLI tool. Uninstalling
the app may leave project attachments and data stored outside its package.

You can review local data, delete conversations using available controls,
choose or disconnect providers and tools, stop recording, revoke microphone
permission in Windows settings, and close the app to stop its activity. For a
complete local cleanup, close the app and remove the app-managed data and
attachments you no longer need. Keep backups of anything you want to retain.
Manage provider credentials, remote history, and deletion requests with the
relevant provider. Local deletion does not delete third-party copies.

Local data is protected by your operating system's access controls. We do not
claim that all project files, conversation history, or logs are encrypted by
this app. Security also depends on your device, selected providers, and tools.
Avoid including information you do not want those services to receive.

If you voluntarily contact support, the information you submit is used to
investigate and respond to your request. Public GitHub issues are visible to
others; do not post credentials or confidential content there.

For privacy questions or requests, contact the publisher through the repository:

This policy will be updated when the Store app's data practices change.
